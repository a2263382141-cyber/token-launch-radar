Assumptions, stated plainly

  • Farm verdicts assume 2% of implied value reaches active farmers, plus $25 of gas. Farm means the net clears $100,000, thin means it clears $25,000, otherwise skip. Change any of those numbers and the verdicts move.
  • Fee capture is a proxy: the snapshot carries no revenue or fee figures, so a category flag (Dexs, Lending, Perpetuals, Yield and similar) stands in for fee-bearing business. A fee-charging protocol in a non-flagged category scores lower than it should.
  • Attention is a proxy too: the snapshot carries no historical watchlist or attention series, so the "peer premium" is the median ln(mcap/TVL) of live-token protocols in the same category — how the market prices that category against TVL.
  • Launch odds are the share of protocols in a category that already have a token, from this snapshot's own record. Categories with fewer than 5 protocols fall back to the global share.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are excluded, as are exchanges and version-marked pools. A protocol is only "pre-token" when the snapshot records no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it.
